
# LexEdu Production

### Educational Technology & Game Development

LexEdu Production is an independent educational technology and game development project based in Kazakhstan.

Our mission is to make education more engaging, accessible, and effective through technology, interactive learning, and digital products.

## Our Areas

- Educational Software
- Educational Games
- Language Learning
- Digital Learning Resources
- Interactive Educational Content

## Our Projects

### Lexpello
A vocabulary learning platform designed to make language acquisition more engaging.

### LexEdu History
Educational materials and interactive learning resources focused on Kazakhstan's history and ENT preparation.

## Our Vision

We aim to combine education, technology, and creativity to build useful digital learning experiences.
